About

R.I. is a Pompano native with a smooth urban sound — late-night R&B with a quiet edge. The music sits between ache and ambition: love, grind, and the math of surviving yourself. Projects like My Thoughts and Solo Leveling map that lane. This is the home base. Press play and stay a while.
